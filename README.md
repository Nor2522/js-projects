### 🌟 Mashaariicda ugu Muhiimsan (Highlights)

#### 1. 🍎 Calorie Counter (New!)
Mashruucan wuxuu muujinayaa awoodda maaraynta xogta iyo nadiifinta input-ka:
* **Regex Sanitization:** Wuxuu sifeeyaa calaamadaha khaldan iyo nambarada sayniska (scientific notation).
* **Dynamic UI:** Waxaad ku dari kartaa cuntooyin aan xad lahayn adiga oo aan bogga dib u cusboonaysiin.
* **Calculated Logic:** Wuxuu si otomaatig ah u xisaabiyaa surplus/deficit adigoo isticmaalaya formula-da: $Remaining = Budget - Consumed + Exercise$.

#### 2. 🌙 Islamic Quiz App
Project-kan wuxuu leeyahay astaamo gaar ah oo aan ku tijaabiyay xirfadayda UI/UX:
* **Shumac Effect:** Progress bar-ka wuxuu leeyahay dab (flame) sii weynaanaya.
* **3D Glassmorphism:** Naqshad muraayad u eg oo casri ah.
